description: Covariance-model search across 2,300+ cyanobacterial genomes

Riboswitches are structured RNA elements that sense small molecules and regulate gene expression. I built a pipeline that uses Infernal covariance models to annotate riboswitches across more than 2,300 cyanobacterial genomes, producing a genome-scale view of which riboswitch classes appear where.

A first-author manuscript describing this work is in preparation.
