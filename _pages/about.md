I work at the intersection of molecular biology and machine learning. My background spans wet-lab molecular biology, computational genomics, and machine learning on biomedical data.

**Research interests**

- **Proteomics modeling.** I build pathway-informed graph models on plasma proteomics data, combining protein language model embeddings with Gene Ontology pathways to predict disease and to show which biological pathways drive the prediction.
- **Genomics.** I built a genome-scale riboswitch annotation pipeline across more than 2,300 cyanobacterial genomes (first-author manuscript in preparation).
- **LLM agents for biology.** I am building agents that query biological databases and the literature, and studying when their conclusions are actually supported by their sources.

Before moving into computation, I did RNAi, RT-qPCR, and western blotting in vaccine R&D, which shapes how I think about where biological data comes from and what it can support.

**Education**

- M.S. and B.S. in Computer Science, Washington University in St. Louis
- B.A. in Molecular Biology, Kenyon College

I am currently looking for research positions in computational biology and biomedical AI.
